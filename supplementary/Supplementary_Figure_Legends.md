# Supplementary figure legends

**Supplementary Figure S1. Detection power for splicing events.** Simulated detection power for a three-versus-three design over observed coverage levels and true ΔPSI values from 0.05 to 0.30. The horizontal line marks 80% power and the vertical line marks |ΔPSI| = 0.10.

**Supplementary Figure S2. Replicate-level assessment of a TRPC1 skipped-exon call.** (A) Individual PSI values with inclusion/skipping junction counts. (B) Bootstrap distribution of ΔPSI from 20,000 resamples; the 95% interval includes zero. (C) Leave-one-out estimates; removing one control replicate reverses the sign. The event is presented as an example of a conventional call that fails the additional robustness criteria.

**Supplementary Figure S3. Replicate-level PSI for SOCE-related splicing candidates.** (A) STIMATE, (B) ORAI3, (C) STIM2 and (D) STIM1 skipped-exon events in the public SH-SY5Y RNA-seq comparison. Each dot is a library; short horizontal lines show group means. Text gives the rMATS junction-count ΔPSI and replicate-level bootstrap 95% interval (10,000 resamples). STIM1's interval includes zero. These events were not measured in the laboratory experiments of Figure 1.

**Supplementary Figure S4. Positive controls and specificity of the annotation-free junction screen.** (A) Literature cryptic-splicing positive controls recovered de novo in SH-SY5Y, with knockdown/control junction-read counts. (B) Genes with a high-confidence unannotated splicing change in each of four cumulative Ca²⁺ panels; the numbers below the tiers are panel sizes, and tiers are not independent sets. (C) Number of literature positive-control genes recovered under the permissive definition in human comparisons, including the FUS and TAF15 knockdown controls. Mouse comparisons are omitted because the literature control events are human cryptic events.

**Supplementary Figure S5. Exploratory NMD interaction.** Condition-level TDP-43-specific interaction estimates for genes with a cryptic junction and the remaining genes, with CBARP marked. The four NMD-inhibition conditions are the units of inference; the TDP-43 factor is confounded with sequencing batch.

**Supplementary Figure S6. Coverage-based alternative-polyadenylation screen and STMN2 control.** (A) Depth-qualified SH-SY5Y intronic and distal 3′UTR index changes with bootstrap 95% intervals. The STIM2 intron 13 unit (†) contains the alternatively spliced STIM2 exon and is not independent evidence for a polyadenylation event. (B) Coverage at both ends of STMN2 intron 2 in each replicate after reference skips are excluded; the open marker is a measured zero at the 3′ end in one knockdown replicate. The indices measure coverage gradients, not poly(A) sites.

**Supplementary Figure S7. TRPC1 expression across neurological comparison cohorts.** Cliff's δ (case minus control) is shown for ALS regions, other neurological disorders, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease and multiple sclerosis. Asterisks mark Benjamini–Hochberg q < 0.05; daggers mark uncorrected p < 0.05 for the donor-level normal-appearing white matter and myelin-adjusted comparisons. Pale bars are not significant. Cohorts and tissue compartments differ, so the display is descriptive across diseases.

**Supplementary Figure S8. STMN2 expression and cryptic junction inclusion in ALS tissue.** (A) Percentage of ALS and non-neurological control samples with a cryptic STMN2 junction, by region, among samples with at least 20 junction reads at the donor; sample counts for each group are printed in the labels. (B, C) Within-ALS Spearman correlations of selected genes with total STMN2 expression and cryptic STMN2 PSI, respectively. Black dots mark q < 0.05 across the stated correction family. The heatmaps use the same colour scale; exact coefficients and q values for all tested genes are in Supplementary Table S9. Total STMN2 expression also tracks neuronal content, whereas cryptic junction PSI more directly reflects TDP-43-dependent RNA processing.
